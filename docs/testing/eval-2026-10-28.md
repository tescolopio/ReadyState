## Draft capture and tighter provenance and electrical rules, 04:07 UTC on 10-31

Code d8e0441, plus one check fix afterward (finding 26). Same replay: Alex's 13 turns, debrief only.

**Changes before the run.**
- **Draft capture** for local evals: off by default, needs the server flag and the eval's header, never on in hosted mode, and written to `eval-results/` (git-ignored). It records each draft in full with each failed check's field and matched text, plus timing, tokens, the evidence and the known story defects.
- **Equipment provenance follows the story in order.** The participant establishes an item in their own words: by claiming it (finding one counts, e.g. "I find my old heater"), by obtaining it, or by mentioning it before the story did. Choosing an offered option, or acting on the story's item without claiming it, establishes nothing.
- **Known story defects are named to the model.** Equipment the story introduced is listed in the debrief briefing as something not to rely on.
- **Electrical claims are classified by sentence.** A sentence about the electrical system fails only if it states a cause, a capacity, a prediction or a generalization. Describing the trip, giving it as the reason for an action ("you called maintenance because the breaker tripped"), saying equipment won't work without power, and advice against overloading circuits all pass, each with a regression test.

## Supply durations from arithmetic, and states in the story-facts note, 10-31 (no model calls)

- **Food:** the stored days.
- **Tolerance:** "about" is allowed: within a quarter day, or 15 %.
- **Fails** as `accuracy:unsupported-supply-duration` when the arithmetic is wrong ("would last about three days" against 1 day; "used up within 12 hours"), or when an input is missing: no stored quantity, no people line, or pets, which change the water rate.

## G11 judge contract revised, and a fresh reserved case set, 10-31 (no model calls)

**Manual review versus protection in the running app.** Manual review judges factual accuracy in the eval only. In the running app, a debrief that passes the implemented checks is published without waiting for a reviewer. So manual review isn't a server-side accuracy gate, and nothing beyond those checks protects factual accuracy in the app today. Earlier sections called manual review "the accuracy gate"; that held only for the eval, and those lines are corrected.

**The revised contract** (`scripts/eval/factual/judge.ts`):
- **One assertion at a time.** Each claim is split into material assertions (actor, action, time, quantity, provenance, cause, state), each with its own verdict and evidence references. The claim verdict is derived from the assertions, not taken from the judge: supported only if every assertion is; contradicted if any is; otherwise unsupported if any is; otherwise unclear.
- **Evidence for every assertion.** A supported or contradicted assertion must cite lines. A story assertion counts only with log lines, not the derived note. An unsupported assertion must list what was searched and what is missing.
- **Invalid results.** Missing or unknown references, a missing search for an unsupported assertion, an inexact quote, or a claim verdict its own assertions don't give make the result invalid. An invalid result is counted apart, neither right nor wrong. In the first run, C07 was judged supported while its explanation described a later restoration; under this contract its time assertion would have had to be marked supported for the result to stand.
- **Note check, separate from the claims.** Every STORY FACTS line is numbered and must be checked against the log. A planted false line has to be reported as inconsistent; judging the claims by the right logged hour doesn't count.
- **The evidence boundary.** A contradicted assertion based on outside knowledge, or without evidence from its basis, counts as unsupported, and the violation is reported. Detection (was the defect flagged?) is reported apart from classification (contradicted versus unsupported).
- **Reasoning effort unchanged** (low), so a run measures the method change alone.

**Cases.**
- **Development:** the 21 Alex cases from the first run.
- **Reserved:** 27 new cases from two other households, with labels fixed before any run (`labels.ts`, committed with this section) and hidden from the judge. A test checks that no label, type, source note or set name reaches a prompt.
  - **Charlotte** (hurricane, 13 turns) and **Priya** (heat wave, 15 turns), both from earlier evals.
  - 11 defects, 12 accurate texts and 4 ambiguous cases.
